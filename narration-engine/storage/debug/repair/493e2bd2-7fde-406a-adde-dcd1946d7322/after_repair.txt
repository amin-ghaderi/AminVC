EXERCISE

React – CV 1. Refactored from JavaScript or make new Take the CV you did in the JavaScript workshop and refactor it in React. If you never made that workshop, just start on a blank slate. By doing this, you will exercise JSX and breaking out parts into components.
2. Add the CV to a React project Choose an organization of folders that pleases you, maybe by separating data from images and components, or by having a CV folder with all related content.
Consider these sections in a CV and make them into components linked on a main CV page:
• summary,
• social links (GitHub, LinkedIn,)
• work experience,
• education,
• skills,
• other relevant information.
